The proSAAS Chaperone is Highly Expressed in Retinal Neurons open access

Schaffer N, Mitias S, Guo Y, Bernstein S, Lindberg I. PLoS ONE 20(5): e0321867. May 16th 2025.

Shows that proSAAS, a protein that helps keep other neural proteins from clumping, is abundant in retinal neurons, pointing to a role in keeping the retina healthy.

ProSAAS is Preferentially Secreted from Neurons During Homeostatic Scaling and Reduces Amyloid Plaque Size in the 5xFAD Mouse Hippocampus

Mitias S, Schaffer N, Nair S, Lindberg I. Journal of Neurochemistry. August 8th 2024.

Neurons release more proSAAS when they rebalance their own activity, and in a mouse model of Alzheimer's disease, proSAAS reduced the size of amyloid plaques.
